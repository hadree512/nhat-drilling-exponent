# Volve exploration wells 15/9-19 (A, B/BT2, S/SR)

`fmt_15_9_19BT2.csv` was transcribed by hand from the scanned "Formation Multi-Tester Pressure Test Summary Report"
(run 1A, trip 1, 18 Jan 1998) in the Volve release, file `wells/15_9-19_B_BT2/legacy_logs/04.tif`.
Only tests marked "good" or "v.good" are kept (tests 8, 9, 25 and 28-32 were tight or had no seal).
Pressures are sand-face pressures in kPa; depths are as printed (m, RKB).

Source: Equinor ASA, Volve field data (Equinor Open Data Licence).
